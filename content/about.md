An app for people who don't follow sports but keep ending up in conversations about them. Each day we tell you the game people will bring up, explain it in a line, and give you something to say.

## How it works

1. Take a quick quiz and get a team, so you have an answer when someone asks.
2. Check Home before you head out. Your team is on top, then what people might mention.
3. Still lost? Ask. We won't tell anyone.

## Who made it

Fake Fanatic, a Coltasa company. Started by someone from New York who got tired of being asked "Jets or Giants?"

## Is it free?

Lite is free and covers the basics: a daily cheat sheet, your team, the top game each week and prep for one person. It has ads. Varsity adds every big game, unlimited people and family groups, full briefings and international clubs.

## Do I need to know anything about sports?

No. Fake Fanatic is made for people who don't follow sports. Everything is explained in plain language.

## Can I change my team?

Yes. Retake the quiz or pick a new team in Settings whenever you like. Nobody has to know.

## When can I get it?

Fake Fanatic is coming soon to iPhone and Android. [Join the waitlist](/#signup) and you'll hear first.

## What do you do with my data?

We use what you tell us, like your team and the people you're prepping for, to build your briefings. We don't sell it. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy) has the details.

## The fine print

Fake Fanatic isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any league, team or player. Team names belong to their owners. Scores and stats come from licensed sports-data partners. Chatbot answers are generated by AI and can be wrong.
